Cranpose with game engines

Cranpose and Bevy render with one WebGPU device in your browser: Bevy's valley inside a Cranpose layout, and a Cranpose HUD over Bevy's camera. The examples need WebGPU (Chrome, Edge, Safari 26).

Bevy inside CranposeCranpose's controls and glass around Bevy's 3D scene.
Cranpose HUD in BevyA Cranpose interface over a Bevy game.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, run with Shift, and drag to look. Source, desktop downloads and project templates: github.com/samoylenkodmitry/cranpose-engines.
